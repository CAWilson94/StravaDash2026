Connect your account to see your weekly training stats

Create a Strava API App

Go to strava.com/settings/api and create a new application. The name can be anything (e.g. My Dashboard). Set the Authorization Callback Domain to localhost.

2

Enter your credentials

3

Authorise your Strava account

Click below. Strava opens in a new tab — click Authorise. You'll land on a page that won't load. That's fine! Copy the full URL from your browser's address bar.

It will look like:

http://localhost/exchange_token?state=&code=abc123xyz&scope=...
4

Paste the redirect URL & connect
